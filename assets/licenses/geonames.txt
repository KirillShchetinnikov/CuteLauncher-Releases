GeoNames geographical database
https://www.geonames.org/

The bundled city catalog is a subset of GeoNames data under the Creative

https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

CuteLauncher selected populated places and administrative seats, combined
city, region and country data, and reduced the fields and alternate names.
The online fallback also uses the GeoNames web service.
